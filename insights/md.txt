# Business Insights

## Project Overview

This analysis explores the Brazilian E-Commerce (Olist) dataset using PostgreSQL and Power BI to uncover sales trends, customer behavior, payment preferences, and delivery performance.

---

## Executive Summary

- Generated over **R$16 million** in total revenue.
- Processed approximately **99,000 customer orders**.
- Served over **96,000 unique customers**.
- Achieved an average order value of approximately **R$161.72**.
- Maintained an average customer review score of **4.08 / 5**.

---

## Key Findings

### 1. Monthly Revenue Trend

Revenue showed steady growth throughout 2017 and peaked during late 2017 to mid-2018, indicating increased customer demand over time.

---

### 2. Top Product Categories

The highest revenue-generating product categories include:

- Watches & Gifts
- Sports & Leisure
- Toys
- Telephony

These categories contributed the largest share of total sales.

---

### 3. Customer Distribution

The majority of customers came from the state of **São Paulo (SP)**, followed by Rio de Janeiro (RJ) and Rio Grande do Sul (RS).

This indicates that customer demand is concentrated in Brazil's more populated regions.

---

### 4. Payment Preferences

Most customers preferred using **Credit Card** as their payment method.

Other payment methods such as Boleto, Voucher, and Debit Card accounted for a much smaller share of total revenue.

---

### 5. Delivery Performance

Orders delivered on time received significantly higher customer review scores than late deliveries.

Average Review Score:

- Early Delivery: **4.29**
- Late Delivery: **2.57**

This suggests that delivery performance has a strong impact on customer satisfaction.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Focus marketing efforts on high-performing product categories.
- Continue investing in regions with the highest customer concentration.
- Improve logistics to reduce late deliveries and increase customer satisfaction.
- Encourage preferred payment methods while evaluating opportunities to improve alternative payment options.
